Our Story

Our Heart & Mission

Our ministry is built on the pillars of restoration, salvation, and healing. We believe in reaching the broken-hearted and providing a sanctuary for those seeking spiritual peace.

Nuestro ministerio se basa en los pilares de la restauración, la salvación y la sanidad. Creemos en llegar a los quebrantados de corazón.

Luke 4:18 | Lucas 4:18

"The Spirit of the Lord is on me, because he has anointed me to proclaim good news to the poor..."

Serving the San Mateo community since our founding, we provide bilingual services that bridge cultures and generations through the love of Christ.

Leadership

Meet Our Pastors

Pastors Ricardo & Ana Molina

Lead Pastors & Community Chaplains

With a deep theological background and a heart for service, Pastors Ricardo and Ana lead Peniel International with a vision of restoration. Pastor Ricardo also serves as a chaplain at Stanford Hospital, bringing spiritual care to patients and families in their most critical moments.

El Pastor Ricardo también sirve como capellán en el Hospital de Stanford, brindando cuidado espiritual a pacientes y familias.
